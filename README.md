# Brennan Robust Unified Terminal Executive (BRUTE)

The **Brennan Robust Unified Terminal Executive**, also known as **BEST**, is a user-friendly terminal to access your **Brennan B2, B3**, and **B3+** backend during support issues. 


***Figure 1**
<img width="1902" height="465" alt="Brute" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/738d4e70-4fac-47d1-9c18-7f3a22d21b4e" />

**Connect**: Input your IP Address in the **Brennan IP** box and click **Connect** or press **Enter**. An IP Address is a set of numbers in a format similar to **192.168.0.207**, and can be found on the Brennan's display

**Scan Subnet**: Attempts to find your IP Address and **Auto-Connects** if it finds your Brennan.

**Clear Output**: Clears the terminal screen.

**Disconnect**: Releases the IP Address and allows another IP Address to be connected during a session run.

**Command**: An area to type any ad hoc commands; press **Send** to commit.

**Reboot**: Reboots your Brennan and reconnects in under a minute. Ideally, reboot once a week, and every time you consider logging a support issue.  Reboots often resolve issues.

**ScanDisk**: This triggers a Brennan **ScanDisk** of your music.  This often resolves issues; therefore, there would be no need to report it unless you have to do this often (more than once a month).

**Sonos**: TBA - Scans for Sonos devices.

**Stats**: Provides information on Brennan Version, Firmware Version, Number of Artists, Albums, tracks, Audio file types, Disk Drive size, etc.

**Drive Stats**: Indepth drive statistic

**Wi-Fi (dB)** Wi-Fi Strength

**DAC Ver**: The DAC version in your Brennan

**Debug Log**: An in-depth log of your Brennan system; the file is saved as **DmesgLog.txt** . Run this if requested and upload or email it to your support tech or post it on your forum post

**Settings**: Saves a copy of the b2settings as **b2settings.txt** file locally, with the Wifi password starred out, like so 2**************7.  Run this if requested and upload or email it to your support tech or post it on your forum post.

**b2db**: Saves a copy of the b2db file locally as **b2db .txt** file. Run this if requested and upload or email it to your support tech or post it on your forum post.

**Export Output**: Saves a copy of the Console output locally as **TerminalOutput.txt**. Run this if requested and upload or email it to your support tech or post it on your forum post.
