# Brennan Robust Unified Terminal Executive (BRUTE)

The **Brennan Robust Unified Terminal Executive**, also known as **BRUTE**, is a user-friendly terminal to access your **Brennan B2, B3**, and **B3+** backend during support issues. 

<img width="1920" height="387" alt="Screenshot_20261105_144224" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/abfa27c1-4471-4921-b612-d2e6dfaff7d8" />



**Connect**: Input your IP Address in the **Brennan IP** box and click **Connect** or press the **Enter** key. An IP Address is a set of numbers in a format similar to **192.168.4.87**, and can be found on the Brennan's display

<img width="1920" height="327" alt="Screenshot_20261105_144631" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/973fdab8-6ac9-41f9-b200-54e43f1f5da9" />



**Scan Subnet**: Attempts to find your IP Address and **Auto-Connects** if it finds your Brennan (on occasion, it may not connect the first time; if so, click and try again).

<img width="1920" height="437" alt="Screenshot_20261105_144802" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d08dd832-704b-4f4b-b158-1a844f2919c3" />



**Clear Output**: Clears the terminal screen content.

<img width="1920" height="430" alt="Screenshot_20261105_145041" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4239eb73-b7c2-453f-a0d3-92a0b3884895" />

**Disconnect**: Releases the IP Address and allows another IP Address to be connected during a session run.

**Command**: An area to type any ad hoc commands; press the **Enter** key or click **Send** to commit.

<img width="1906" height="59" alt="Screenshot_20261105_152844" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6ccc881b-bfb6-4c71-860c-f721f66af677" />


**Reboot**: Reboots your Brennan and reconnects in under a minute. Ideally, reboot once a week, and every time you consider logging a support issue.  Reboots often resolve issues.
<img width="772" height="295" alt="Screenshot_20261105_145408" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a0b4cffd-faee-49c1-94d8-c84af48de3e7" />

**ScanDisk**: This triggers a Brennan **ScanDisk** of your music.  This often resolves issues; therefore, there is no need to report it unless you have to do this often (more than once a month).

The following four buttons export various logs to .txt files

<img width="577" height="55" alt="Screenshot_20261105_153221" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d08fc257-ed72-4403-bb84-c38157ba5c0f" />

**Debug Log**: An in-depth log of your Brennan system; the file is saved as **DmesgLog.txt** . Run this if requested and upload or email it to your support tech or post it on your forum post

**Settings**: Saves a copy of the b2settings as **b2settings.txt** file locally, with the Wifi password starred out, like so 2**************j.  Run this if requested and upload or email it to your support tech or post it on your forum post.

**b2db**: Saves a copy of the b2db file locally as **b2db .txt** file. Run this if requested and upload or email it to your support tech or post it on your forum post.

**Export Output**: Saves a copy of the Console output locally as **TerminalOutput.txt**. Run this if requested and upload or email it to your support tech or post it on your forum post.
